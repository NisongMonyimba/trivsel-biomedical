Assets do not exist alone.

A physical asset sits inside relationships: manufacturer, model, location, custodian, maintenance, calibration, interventions and lifecycle events.

02 / OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

Ask questions grounded in asset context.

Which assets require maintenance attention?Which assets have unresolved interventions?Which records are missing supporting evidence?Illustrative questions; not a claim about every deployment configuration.

03 / READINESS INTELLIGENCE

Understand what blocks readiness.

Connect requirements, current state, unresolved issues and evidence gaps while keeping customer-specific definitions customer-specific.

04 / HUMAN + MACHINE INTELLIGENCE

The system should strengthen human work.

Engineers, technicians, operators and scientists create the operational knowledge. Machine intelligence should help organize and surface it while keeping human responsibility visible.
