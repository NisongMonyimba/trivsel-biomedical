TRIVSEL / PRIVACY

Website and inquiry privacy notice

How information is handled when you visit our public website or contact us about Trivsel.

Draft pending operator and processing verification. Last revised: 19 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible and what this notice covers

Trivsel is a founder-led project in Ghana. The proposed accountable website operator is Nisong Monyimba. For website privacy questions, the existing contact is nisong@trivsel.uk. This notice covers the public website at www.trivsel.uk and related correspondence, not hospital application records.

The accountable operator and correspondence address are not yet verified. Trivsel has not registered with Ghana's Data Protection Commission. This draft is for review only; applicable registration and lawful processing requirements must be resolved before launch or expanded collection.

2. Information you choose to share

If you email us, your message may contain your name, email address, organization, professional role, inquiry details and email metadata. The inquiry pathway uses your email client. Sending an inquiry is voluntary. Without suitable contact information, we may be unable to respond.

Please send only the business contact details and high-level equipment management context needed for an initial discussion. Do not send patient information, passwords, medical records, staff databases, sensitive attachments or confidential hospital records.

3. Why information may be processed

The intended purposes are to respond to your inquiry, discuss prospective pilots or collaborations, maintain relevant correspondence, operate and protect the website, and meet applicable legal obligations. An inquiry does not authorize a hospital pilot or give permission to process patient information.

Separate review is required for any additional purpose or hospital data processing. We will not treat an inquiry alone as consent to unrelated marketing.

4. Website operation and technical information

Website delivery can involve IP addresses, request times, browser or device information and technical diagnostics. Hosting and security services may process this information. Browser storage, cookies, analytics and technical logging practices are being reviewed; this notice does not claim that the website is free of tracking or cookies.

5. Service providers and international processing

Email, website hosting and related technical services may process information to deliver these functions. Correspondence may be accessed from Ghana. Processing can occur across national borders, and local laws may apply in the relevant locations. Do not assume that information is stored exclusively in the United States, Canada or Ghana.

Render is the stated website hosting provider. Its configured hosting region, the email provider, processing locations, access arrangements and applicable safeguards have not yet been verified. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. No hospital-specific transfer arrangement is established by this notice.

6. Retention and safeguards

The proposed retention approach is to keep inquiry information only as needed for the relevant discussion, agreed follow-up, security needs or applicable legal obligations. The proposed retention target for closed or unsuccessful inquiries is six months from closure, subject to lawful exceptions. Specific retention periods for other information, implementation of deletion procedures, backups and safeguards require verification before this notice is finalized. Email is not an approved channel for sensitive hospital records.

7. Your privacy choices and requests

Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to access or correct information, request deletion or restriction, object to processing, withdraw consent where relied upon, or complain to a relevant privacy authority. The contact above is intended for privacy requests. The request-handling procedure remains subject to verification. Appropriate identity verification and legal exceptions may apply.

Do not include sensitive information in the initial request. In Ghana, information about privacy rights and complaints is available from the Data Protection Commission. In Canada, you can consult the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the relevant provincial authority.

8. Hospital pilots and updates

Live hospital use requires separate institutional authorization, data protection assessment and appropriate agreements. Patient-identifiable clinical information is excluded from the initial pilot scope. See the Trust Center for our assurance program. This notice is not a statement of regulatory compliance or an authorization for live hospital processing.

Material changes to the notice should be communicated as appropriate. The revision date identifies this version.
