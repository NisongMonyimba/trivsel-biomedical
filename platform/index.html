TRIVSEL / PLATFORM

Understand the complete life of every asset.

Trivsel organizes the relationships around a physical asset so operational records remain connected as the asset moves, changes responsibility and accumulates history.

PlatformIdentityAsset 360MaintenanceCalibrationInterventionsReadinessHistory

01 / ASSET IDENTITY

Give every physical asset a persistent identity.

Identity lets the rest of the lifecycle attach to the same physical object instead of becoming disconnected records.

IdentityPublic asset ID / QR
Technical contextType, manufacturer, model, serial
Ownership contextOrganization, department, custodian

02 / ASSET 360

Bring operational context into one view.

Asset 360 connects identity, placement, condition, requirements and history around the same physical object.

IdentityPersistent record
LocationDepartment / room

CalibrationEvidence + due dates
InterventionsProblems + actions

03 / LOCATION & RESPONSIBILITY

Know where an asset belongs — and the context around it.

Placement, department and responsibility change. Trivsel treats those changes as part of the asset's operational story.

04 / MAINTENANCE

Connect requirements, work and evidence.

Preventive maintenance becomes more useful when due requirements, completed events and supporting evidence remain connected to the asset.

05 / CALIBRATION

Keep measurement context close to the asset.

Calibration requirements, results, evidence and future due dates form a coherent lifecycle record rather than separate files.

06 / FAULTS & INTERVENTIONS

Record the problem, response and resolution.

Keep the reported problem, acknowledgement, actions and resolution in context.

07 / READINESS

Bring the evidence together before it matters.

Readiness brings maintenance, calibration, unresolved interventions and evidence gaps into view. Customer-specific definitions remain customer-specific.
