PLATFORM / CALIBRATION

Calibration evidence should stay connected to the asset that depends on it.

Trivsel connects calibration status, due-state and supporting evidence to the physical asset itself, so organizations can understand whether a calibration requirement is current, what evidence exists and what action should happen next.

Explore faults & interventions Discuss a pilot

WHY ORGANIZATIONS SHOULD CARE

A calibration record is only useful when the organization can connect it to the correct physical asset and its current operating context.

Calibration can affect whether a measurement-capable asset is ready for use, due for attention or carrying evidence that needs review. A connected record helps teams see calibration as part of the asset's operational state instead of as a detached certificate or spreadsheet row.

See current calibration state

Keep calibration status visible with the asset instead of asking teams to reconstruct it from separate records.

Keep evidence traceable

Connect supporting calibration evidence to the same physical asset so people can find the record that explains the current state.

Understand due-state

Make the next calibration date or due condition easier to review in context with the asset and its operational state.

Support readiness decisions

Calibration status becomes one part of the evidence teams can review before deciding whether an asset is ready for its intended use.

CALIBRATION IS NOT MAINTENANCE

They are connected, but they answer different operational questions.

Maintenance records what was serviced, repaired or adjusted. Calibration records whether a measurement-related requirement has been evaluated and what evidence supports the resulting calibration state.

MAINTENANCE

What work was done to the asset?

Service activity, repairs, replacement, inspection or adjustment belong in the maintenance history.

CALIBRATION

What evidence supports the asset's calibration state?

Calibration status, date, due-state and supporting evidence belong in a calibration record connected to the asset.

ANATOMY OF A CALIBRATION RECORD

The record should explain more than a date.

A useful calibration record connects the physical asset, calibration event, status, due-state and supporting evidence so another person can understand what the current state means.

01

Asset identity

The exact physical asset to which the calibration record applies.

02

Calibration event

The relevant calibration date, activity or evaluation recorded for the asset.

03

Status + due-state

The current calibration state and the next date or condition requiring attention.

04

Supporting evidence

The record, certificate, note or other evidence that explains the calibration event and resulting state.

WHO CARES ABOUT CALIBRATION?

The same calibration evidence supports different decisions across the organization.

ENGINEERS & TECHNICIANS

See status before working with the asset

Review calibration history and due-state alongside maintenance and fault context.

QUALITY & METROLOGY

Keep evidence connected to the correct equipment

Trace the calibration record back to the exact asset instead of relying on disconnected filenames or lists.

OPERATIONS & LABS

Understand whether calibration needs attention

See calibration state as part of the asset's operating context before use or scheduling.

ASSET MANAGEMENT

Carry calibration history through the lifecycle

Keep calibration events attached to the asset as it moves, changes placement or accumulates history.

PROCUREMENT

See calibration requirements around the installed base

Understand which kinds of assets carry calibration obligations when planning replacement or standardization.

LEADERSHIP

Review calibration state without rebuilding the evidence trail

Follow the connection from asset to calibration state to supporting evidence in one operating context.

THE CALIBRATION LOOP

Calibration becomes more useful when every step updates the asset's operational context.

  1. 01Identify the asset

    Start with the exact physical unit whose calibration state matters.

  2. 02Review the requirement

    Understand the relevant calibration need, timing or due-state.

  3. 03Record the event

    Capture the calibration activity and resulting status.

  4. 04Attach the evidence

    Keep supporting records connected to the same asset.

  5. 05Update readiness context

    Carry the new calibration state into the asset's broader operational history.

CONNECTED CALIBRATION

Calibration becomes operational intelligence when evidence, due-state and asset history stay connected.

Trivsel places calibration inside the wider physical-asset operating model. Identity tells you which asset the record belongs to. Context explains where and how that asset is used. Calibration adds evidence and state. History preserves what changed. People can then decide what should happen next.

Asset→Context→Calibration→History→Action
